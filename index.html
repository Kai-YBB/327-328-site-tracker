Each record comes from Boston Employment Commission minutes or agendas and describes a project, not a signed agreement with the union. A contractor on a union project is a lead, not proof of a letter with Local 327 or 328. To check whether a contractor is signatory, call the local office listed on the Locals tab.

Checking if a contractor is signatory

NASRCC's searchable contractor database is open only to signatory contractors, so there is no public list of letter holders. The local office can tell you if a contractor is signatory.
